## How decisions are made

kscope is a small project with a lead maintainer. Day to day:

* Changes land through pull requests reviewed by a maintainer. CI must be green.
* Design questions are discussed in the issue or in
  [Discussions](https://github.com/hknerts/kscope/discussions) before large
  changes are written. The project lead has the final say when there is no
  consensus.
* The one non-negotiable rule is that kscope stays **read-only** (see
  [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md#the-one-rule)).

## Becoming a maintainer

Contributors who have landed several non-trivial pull requests and reviewed
others' work may be invited to become maintainers, with commit access. Open an
issue or ask in Discussions if you would like to help maintain an area such as
packaging, fleets or the log pipeline.
